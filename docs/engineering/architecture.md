Repository contracts and export format preserve portability. SGD minor units only in payment path, no Stripe FX; future currency/corridor modeled separately. Private uploads scanned, signed links, RBAC, retention, provenance. No secret or raw receipt in agent analytics.

## Stripe Connect direct-charge and timing gate
Single provider, direct charges on dedicated verified traveller connected accounts; prefer stable Accounts v2 or v1 controllers with Stripe-managed pricing/risk and Stripe-hosted onboarding where eligible. No SCT/destination fallback without new founder decision. Actual account configuration, legal merchant role and provider permissions require approval. No shopper principal in platform balance, no wallets, no escrow claim or arbitrary bank debits. No funded rematching. Read direct-charge-acceptance-v0.11.md; all applicable mandatory criteria are release blockers, not optional optimisations.
Narrow server-side payment adapter (create direct checkout, retrieve connected account/charge, refund, fee refund, approved platform-funded reimbursement, payout, reconcile). Pin connected account and immutable order/merchant to each payment, verify signed Connect events and retrieve authoritative state in same account and environment; no client-supplied account override. Store versioned account control fingerprint, provider-authoritative capabilities, payout limits and deadlines. Dedicated account or proven provider-supported isolation of GetViaMe obligations; manual payout exclusively under authorised platform control, prohibit merchant-initiated manual/instant/API/automatic bypass. Check controls before funding and payout, monitor drift, deauthorisation and capability changes; quarantine new business on missing evidence while keeping refunds and obligations operational through approved fallback.
Separate charge/refund/application-fee refund/reimbursement/payout objects and ledgers, platform-owned reserve and fee accounting, available balance reconciliation per account, no use of unrelated customer principal. Atomic per-account/order reservation for refund, dispute and payout with transactional outbox and provider idempotency; unknown response retrieved/reconciled before retry. Payout cap = eligible unpaid obligations limited by actual available balance after reserving all ineligible obligations, owed/pending refunds and other approved liabilities/adjustments; if inconsistency freeze affected payout, alert, preserve owed refunds. Provider fees/tax and rounding must match approved seller net. No release before 30+7+7 rules and cleared disputes. Never assume Stripe-managed risk funds refunds. Independently fund shortfall from platform resources including cold start, no reliance on future customer revenue. Approved late/failed refund fallback to verified original payer, human exception approval, no duplicate and no wallet.
Durable tasks for 30-day delivery maximum, actual report +3/+6/+7 days, confirmation +7 days, provider cutoff alerts, weekly eligible cash payout; never backdate to shorten rights. Separate provider payout acceptance from bank-paid confirmation. Failure/offboarding/closure/disconnect plan must preserve outstanding obligations, even when API access is lost; no live onboarding until verified. Cards deferred; promotions flag OFF until approved platform subsidy and legal classification. Safety gates enforced server-side, not bypassable by feature flags or admin/AI overrides; configuration changes require regression evidence.
