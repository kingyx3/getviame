# Payment provider selection diligence — v0.8, 2026-11-28

Research recommendation only; main 7e26b982e2c199e883744545b0e99da390254612, draft PR #2 and owner-answers-v0.8.md supersede stale wallet specs. No provider chosen, quoted, contacted or cleared. Public docs do not establish a definitive all-in cheapest eligible provider.

## Mandatory gates
SGD PayNow collection; legally eligible occasional individual travellers (KYC), provider-controlled order-linked funds without GetViaMe customer wallet or own-bank pooling; confirmed funding before purchase; no seller payout before delivery confirmation + separate seven-day dispute period (ordinary 44 days from collection, weekly payout thereafter, potentially longer with disputes/rematch); full and partial original-tender refunds including late exception; 9% inclusive min SGD1, platform absorbs processor fees, 20% post-valid purchase receipt penalty inclusive fee; exact seller payout; immutable transaction/fee IDs and reconciliation; consent-based same-request rematch within original request expiry AND permitted retention/refund deadlines; provider-approved replacement recipient reallocation, partial price change and no clock reset. Referral discounts can fund up to 100% of eligible order from platform money; off until funding mechanism cleared. Deferred card authorisation at offer selection/capture at purchase receipt is future only. PayNow default remains recommended pending Q122; Q124/Q126 not explicitly answered.

## Cost model
Illustrative 100 paid SGD100 orders, 20 fully refunded before purchase receipt, 80 completed, distinct 80 active travellers and 80 weekly payouts, no promotions. Gross fees SGD720, total seller payout SGD7280. Stripe platform pricing: collection 130, active 160, payouts 58.20 before rounding per payout (58.40 if each 0.7275 rounded to 0.73); total approx SGD348.20–348.40; remaining approx 371.60–371.80 before GST and other expenses. HitPay retail collection only 95; Omise 115; Xendit 160; cannot compare these to all-in Stripe as if payout/holding services free. Airwallex wins this benchmark if complete comparable cost below approx SGD348/month, i.e. 3.48 per funded order, all else equal. Negotiation target <=1% collection and no fixed/active account fees, not an actual quotation. At 10 SGD order, Stripe collection + active + one payout approx 2.65 exceeds SGD1 gross fee; not all small orders profitable even with weekly batches. Track cost by payout density, cancellation, order sizes and actual taxes, reserves, refund fees and platform minimums. No unilateral pricing change.

## Selection and acceptance
Recommendation Airwallex provisional first choice, Stripe baseline/fallback, HitPay and Omise pricing challengers if core capability gaps closed, Adyen optional quote. No definitive winner without written commercial and compliance approval; do not weaken 44-day/dispute or individual requirements. Ask each to demonstrate successful SGD100: hold beyond day44, full refund day40/late, 80 refund + 11 seller compensation + 9 gross fee less processor costs, refund/replace seller, open dispute past expiry, weekly exact net payout, full promo funding (from platform) and reconciliation replay; test same request rematch and no arbitrary wallet. Clarify regulatory perimeter and MoR/custody, contractual maximum retention, refund cutoffs, price/fees and minimums, KYC per attempt, funding/negative balance/reserves, signed webhook status. Provider contact, live/sandbox testing, git status/npm check/strict validation not performed; no code or deployment. Main still stale; PR #2 draft. No confidential quote or secrets included.
