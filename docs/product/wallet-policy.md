# Individual SGD credit wallets — proposed policy v0.3

Owner wants per-user credits and an incentive to recycle spend. This is a compliance-gated product specification, not a claim that Stripe Connect provides wallet services.

| Source | Intended use | Proposed restriction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Refund principal | Elect credit instead of eligible original-method refund | Spend on future orders, no forced election; do not expire or confiscate principal pending legal policy |
| Earned principal | Elect credits instead of scheduled cash payout | Spend on orders; cash withdrawal/reversal rights to confirm |
| Earnings incentive | +2% from fee, proposed 2 percentage points of gross | Spend-only, expiry/reversal rules to confirm |
| Referral credit | S$10 reward | Spend-only, 180 days, minimum S$50 gross order; stacking/co-pay pending |
| Cash payable | Traveller cash settlement | Provider scheduled payout; not spendable wallet balance |

No top-up, peer transfer, arbitrary currency conversion or unrestricted withdrawal without explicit approval. Separate sources in journal even if UI combines balances; display available, reserved and pending, terms and eligibility. Amounts SGD cents; backing/liability reconciled to approved licensed provider; no unsupported custody in platform bank/Stripe balance. Refund credit is not a Stripe refund. Original fees sunk; future payment fees may be avoided only on amount covered by credits. No cash refund and credit for same principal. Informed opt-in, original-method refund when required or chosen; no compulsory credit. Mixed tender refunds restore the source-specific balance or original tender under approved rules. Never convert promotional credits to cash via cancellation, self-trade, or refund. No automatic expiry of customer principal without lawful approved policy.

Illustrative S$100 fulfilled order: cash S$92, gross platform fee S$8; credit election proposed S$92 earned principal + S$2 incentive, gross retained fee S$6 before processor/payout/other costs. Alternative 2% of net S$93.84 unresolved. No double cash/credit payout; record election and consent before settlement, proposed lock at settlement eligibility. Bonus only on normal completed eligible orders (proposed), not refund/penalty. For S$100 shopper cancellation after eligible receipt, proposed gross-base S$20 compensation to traveller and S$80 refunded to shopper, no platform share; fees borne by platform, legal/evidence exceptions apply. Mixed-tender and fee accounting to finalize.

Provider/legal approval must cover issuer, custody/safeguarding, account issuance/e-money, KYC/AML, refund rights, expiry, settlement and limits. No live wallet or automatic refund election until approved. Agent tests use synthetic funds, no real money. A cash-only interim pilot is a separate owner decision.
