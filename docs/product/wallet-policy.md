# Wallet-free payment and promotional policy — v0.9
Q121 removes shopper refund wallets and Q73/79 reversal removes earnings credits/bonus. This file replaces the former wallet policy; historical versions remain in Git and owner evidence. No customer-money wallet, top-up, P2P, reusable refund principal, withdrawal account or earnings credit. A displayed settlement history is informational, not spendable. Stripe Connect selected, actual financial structure gated.
Monetary refunds return to original tender, with approved verified alternate return only when original-method refund is impossible and law/provider permit. For cancellation before valid purchase receipt or traveller cancellation: full applicable refund, zero fee/compensation; after valid receipt: 20% gross penalty inclusive 9% fee (minimum S$1), 80% refund, compensation penalty minus fee. Restore eligible promotional amount proportionately; never convert promotions to cash. Use integer cents and half-up fee/penalty rounding; exact proportional remainder and expiry-on-restoration rules proposed for approval. No arbitrary principal expiry. GetViaMe bears ordinary processor costs; separately funded reserve and fee records.
Same-request funded rematch with explicit shopper consent until original request expiry subject to provider/legal deadlines, no reset of original payment date. Refund on withdrawal or deadline; request can remain open unfunded. No general credit balance or silent reassignment.
Promotions are platform-funded discounts, never paid-in value: S$10 registration grant and S$10 pending referrer reward on first qualifying >=S$50 order with actual customer-origin payment, unlock after successful completion/dispute clearance, cap 15, 180 days per issued grant. Stack eligible grants up to 100% gross on orders >=S$50, no artificial co-pay for redemption. Stripe PayNow minimum S$0.50 may require explicit shopper adjustment if residual cash is 1–49 cents; proposed handling in launch gates, not approved change. Reserve full commitment within S$500 tranches, pause new enrolments, honour existing promises. Referral flag OFF until counsel and provider approve classification, platform funding, reversals and fraud checks. Promo-only S$50 order needs S$45.50 platform cash for seller (before fees), not fictitious provider collection. No live financial capability before signoff.
