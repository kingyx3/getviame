## New findings (official documentation)
- Standard/Express/Custom are legacy presets, not recommended new integration account types; use Accounts v2 configurable dashboard, roles, fee and loss responsibility where required capabilities are supported on stable API, otherwise v1 controller properties. https://docs.stripe.com/connect/accounts ; https://docs.stripe.com/connect/integration-recommendations ; https://docs.stripe.com/connect/accounts-v2/connected-account-configuration ; https://docs.stripe.com/connect/migrate-to-controller-properties . v2 recipient configuration for receiving transfers is distinct from limited-country recipient service agreement, do not conflate. https://docs.stripe.com/connect/accounts-v2 ; https://docs.stripe.com/connect/service-agreement-types .
- Legacy Standard direct charges, full dashboard, Stripe KYC and connected-account unrecoverable loss responsibility, no additional Connect fees where Stripe bills seller. Express limited dashboard, Stripe-hosted KYC, platform risk; Custom no hosted dashboard, greater platform compliance/support effort. Legacy Express and Custom fee behaviors differ from explicit application fee-payer controller. https://docs.stripe.com/connect/direct-charges-fee-payer-behavior .
- Stripe explicitly recommends Express/embedded dashboard + platform fees and losses for destination/separate charges. Direct charges recommended with Stripe loss responsibility, either fee collector. Selecting Stripe as direct charge fee collector avoids Connect account/payout charges under published standard model, but it does not make platform-level charges or indirect-charge fees free. https://stripe.com/en-sg/connect/pricing ; https://docs.stripe.com/connect/integration-recommendations ; https://docs.stripe.com/connect/direct-charges-fee-payer-behavior . Do not promise no Connect fees for SCT by setting recipient fee collector to Stripe contrary to documented marketplace integration.
- Managed Risk available for direct charges with Stripe KYC, Radar, card_payments capability and full agreement (even if PayNow only at checkout); embedded-only option documented. Express dashboard + Stripe loss responsibility explicitly public preview with 2026-07-29.preview header, not stable MVP dependency; fee collector Stripe combination/SG eligibility needs verification. https://docs.stripe.com/connect/risk-management/managed-risk . Stripe loss responsibility covers unrecoverable connected-account negative balances only, not platform obligations or negative platform balance. Stripe-managed-risk Express requires certain connected-account controls including refunds/disputes/top-up refund balance, cannot disable. https://docs.stripe.com/connect/customize-express-dashboard .
- Standard platform controls can change payout interval, but restricting a user's schedule does NOT stop their manual payouts; Stripe explicitly requires further restriction for full control. https://docs.stripe.com/connect/platform-controls-for-stripe-dashboard-accounts . Express dashboard also permits manual payouts where available; customization and full restrictions must be verified, not assume label guarantees exclusive control. Embedded-only may reduce exposure but required risk-management interfaces remain; account closure, bank changes, off-platform activity must not defeat settlement gates. https://docs.stripe.com/connect/express-dashboard ; https://docs.stripe.com/connect/customize-express-dashboard .
- Stripe expressly permits delayed manual payouts for delivery/refund scenarios, 90-day Singapore connected-account limit; automated settlement delay override only up to 31 days on eligible configurations, insufficient for 44-day lifecycle. https://docs.stripe.com/connect/manual-payouts ; https://docs.stripe.com/connect/manage-payout-schedule . Stripe payouts guide also expressly lists retaining funds in platform balance for indirect charges; technical permission not proof of case-specific permitted duration or legal safeguarding. https://docs.stripe.com/connect/payouts-connected-accounts . Use manual payout on eligible obligations, or delay SCT transfer until eligible then approved weekly payout; not merely weekly auto-payout on all direct-charge funds. PayNow all three types, 1.3%, 90-day native refund from charge date, T+1 availability default for PayNow (distinct from general initial/standard payout disclosures), no auth, 1-hour QR vs conditional 15-minute reservation. https://docs.stripe.com/payments/paynow .
- NEW potential connected-account risk-reserve API: preview holds/plans, transaction-linked, release manually and extend date up to 180 days from hold creation. Automatic release at scheduled date/180d or sufficiently large refund/dispute; partial refund below hold does not automatically release and can cause negative balance. https://docs.stripe.com/connect/connected-account-reserves . No verified SG PayNow eligibility, compatibility with chosen risk responsibility, permitted 100% seller allocation or override of 90-day payout deadline. Risk reserves not general escrow; do not rely on preview or misuse risk facility for general custody. Research candidate only.
- NEW promo funding caveat: Connect platform top-ups for seller transfers lists general availability US/UK/JP, private preview EU/CA/AU/NZ, Singapore not listed. https://docs.stripe.com/connect/top-ups . General refund/dispute prefunding guide lists SG wire transfers; different balance/purpose, not evidence of seller-transfer funding availability. https://docs.stripe.com/get-started/account/add-funds . Approved retained earned platform revenue or provider-approved funding mechanism needed for subsidies, ordinary cancellation shortfalls and fee rebates; no use of customer principal, no fake charge. Referral remains off until funded path proven.
- MAS licence and SG safeguarding disclosure https://eservices.mas.gov.sg/fid/institution/detail/219107-STRIPE-PAYMENTS-SINGAPORE-PTE-LTD ; https://stripe.com/en-sg/legal/singapore-disclosures . Stripe direct charges place money in traveller Stripe account, potential lower regulatory exposure for platform vs platform charge, not exemption. Direct charge MoR is connected account; indirect on_behalf_of connected account, otherwise platform. https://docs.stripe.com/connect/merchant-of-record . ACRA own-full-name registration exemption where applicable https://www.acra.gov.sg/register/business/requirements-eligibility/ ; not proof Stripe approves every individual or traveller eligible to work. Stripe 2026 verification updates distinguish non-individual accounts https://support.stripe.com/questions/2026-updates-to-singapore-verification-requirements .

## Candidate recommendation
1. Preserve founder's rematch requirement: modern Express-equivalent, Stripe hosted onboarding, application fee/loss responsibility, SG recipient transfer capability and verified individual identity, SCT with no seller transfer until eligible, weekly payouts; stable v2 if fully supported else v1 controllers; no extra provider. Published marketplace approach https://docs.stripe.com/connect/marketplace . No verified per-order funds isolation (SG PayNow not eligible for segregation preview), actual MoR/custody, hold, dispute and late refunds still external gates.
2. Lower-cost alternative: direct charges on individual merchant accounts, Stripe fees/losses, hosted onboarding and embedded-only or full Dashboard if exclusive manual payout controls proven. Express Stripe-loss preview not recommended for MVP. Direct charges bind seller at collection, do not support reassignment of original charge; requires founder approval to refund/recollect instead of funded rematch and legal review of genuine seller MoR. Fee payer Stripe: illustrative SGD100, processing 1.30, application fee 7.70 instead of 9 to leave traveller 91. For full cancellation return application fee in full and reimburse seller 1.30 via approved platform-funded route; partial 80 refund leaves 20-1.30-fee7.70=11 if fee unchanged; fee based on actual confirmed costs and taxes, adjust appropriately. No fee shifted to seller. Direct-charge application fee refunds are not automatic unless requested, and no negative application fee possible; refund/fee subsidy must be tested. https://docs.stripe.com/connect/direct-charges.md?platform=web&ui=stripe-hosted . Do not infer subsidy transfers free or supported from standard no-Connect-fee model.
3. Destination charges with seller balance hold less compelling; initial transfer occurs immediately, reversals and reallocation could be technically possible but need permission and sufficient balance, not silently change merchant or settlement rule.
4. Legacy Custom adds complexity and liability without lower published Connect cost, not recommended.
