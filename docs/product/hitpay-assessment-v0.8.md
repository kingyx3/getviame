Research only. Main 7e26b982e2c199e883744545b0e99da390254612; draft PR #2; founder decisions in owner-answers-v0.7/v0.8.md. No vendor selection or change to product scope. Latest v0.8 supersedes stale main wallet rules.

## Verified public evidence
- MAS directory: HitPay Payment Solutions Pte Ltd is MPI for domestic/cross-border money transfer and merchant acquisition, NOT listed for account/e-money issuance. https://eservices.mas.gov.sg/fid/institution/detail/250313-HITPAY-PAYMENT-SOLUTIONS-PTE-LTD . Wallet removal makes scope more plausible but does not exempt GetViaMe from licensing; provider contract and actual roles/control matter. https://www.mas.gov.sg/regulation/payments/licensing-for-payment-service-providers .
- Platform offering explicitly serves registered businesses; each submerchant verified, payouts to own bank on standard schedule, not pooled by platform. https://hitpayapp.com/sg/platforms ; https://docs.hitpayapp.com/platforms/overview . Individual unregistered traveller eligibility and order-specific conditional settlement through dispute clearance are not established. Standard payouts are not equivalent to protected order funds; risk reserves held by provider are not a platform-controlled release feature. https://hitpayapp.com/sg/payment-account-held .
- NEW verified blocker: HitPay refund docs last modified 2026-10-28 list Singapore PayNow QR maximum refund period 30 days, instant within period, original processing fees not returned. Requires sufficient merchant balance; suggests direct transfer for expired refund window. https://docs.hitpayapp.com/payments/refund . GetViaMe has up to 44 days ordinary acceptance-to-dispute end plus extensions/rematching. Require provider-approved verified original-payer late-refund workflow, not arbitrary manual bank transfers or customer wallet; do not shorten approved dispute rights.
- Platform Key supports fixed PayNow commission per payment, so min SGD1 fee possible for cash-only orders. However docs state processing fees and platform commission both deducted from submerchant proceeds, and commission received upon payment. https://docs.hitpayapp.com/apis/guide/platform-apis (modified 2026-07-07). SGD100 with SGD9 commission and SGD0.95 collection fee -> seller SGD90.05, not promised SGD91. Need provider-approved fee allocation or adjustment, delayed/reversible commission, partial/full refund allocation, promotional subsidy and mixed tender reconciliation. Not authorising changing commission or passing costs to travellers.
- HitPay published domestic payout API capability https://hitpayapp.com/ph/blog/payout-apis-disbursements does not establish that it can retain third-party funds under marketplace terms. Ordinary merchant account with funds paid to GetViaMe then mass disbursed is not approved.
- Retail online PayNow below SGD100 max(0.20,0.9%); >=100 0.65%+0.30. No additional standard refund fee; original fee retained. https://hitpayapp.com/sg/pricing . Stripe PayNow 1.3% https://stripe.com/en-sg/pricing/local-payment-methods . SGD100 costs SGD0.95 vs SGD1.30, saving SGD0.35/collection; 1000 such paid orders -> SGD350; SGD10 HitPay SGD0.20 vs Stripe SGD0.13. Excludes GST, marketplace fees, payouts, top-up/late refund costs and other charges; no negotiated quotes. Do not apply in-person rates to online checkout.
- Airwallex individual SG onboarding and manual-release split docs https://www.airwallex.com/docs/connected-accounts/onboarding/kyc-and-onboarding/native-api/individual-kyc-requirements/sg ; https://www.airwallex.com/docs/payments-for-platforms/airwallex-gateway/manage-funds-split . More documented fit, not confirmed contractual clearance.

## Recommendation and gates
Invite HitPay alongside Airwallex and Stripe for single-provider RFQ. Require written support for unregistered occasional individual travellers (subject to applicable law), provider-held order funds for 44+ days and open disputes, weekly payout, verified late refund beyond 30 days, consent-based order-specific rematch, full and partial commission reversal, platform absorbs fees and finances discounts, reconciliation and legal perimeter. If core gates fail, do not use standard merchant gateway and own bank account as substitute; choose compliant alternative. No requirement for e-money issuer in revised architecture, but payment-provider and legal approvals remain. Funded rematch separate feature gate; if unapproved refund/recollect. No vendor contacted, test executed, legal determination, implementation or deployment. Existing draft requirements still need reconciliation; this research does not change approved behaviour.
