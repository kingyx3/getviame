# Logical domain model v0.11 (not physical schema)

User: Google subject/issuer, locale, indicative display currency, both roles, eligibility; provider KYC separate. Product parent → Variant → RegionalListing; image, brand, attributes, region/exclusivity, retailer, provenance. PriceObservation (variant, region, currency, amount, source user/public/receipt, time, tax basis, confidence, verification, visibility); unknown MSRP distinct from zero; private receipt-derived observations not public. 
Request: shopper, variant snapshot, source/destination, quantity, SGD total >= 1000 minor units, deadline/window, conditional food expiry, moderation. Trip: legs, origin/destination, dates, optional city/airport, visibility, no capacity. Offer: traveller, request, optional trip, dates, total SGD, expiry and terms version; multiple counteroffers, one active accepted order, historical cancelled orders retained. No self trade or partial quantity in MVP.
Order: immutable accepted snapshot, price/fee/currency, parties, quantity, accepted_at, delivery_deadline <= accepted_at + 30 days, acknowledged terms; revisions by consent recorded separately. Traveller bears costs. ConnectedAccount: verified traveller/provider account ID, capabilities, contractual role, approved configuration fingerprint, payout control state, account status and restriction evidence. PaymentAttempt/ProviderEvent/ApplicationFee/FeeRefund/Reimbursement/Refund/Payout: connected account and charge IDs, amount, currency, dedupe, authoritative status. Immutable balanced journal distinguishes connected-account proceeds/obligations and platform-owned cash/fee/subsidy reserve; no user-money wallet. PaymentAllocation links immutable direct charge and original merchant/order, collection time, tender, provider refund/payout limits, actual fee, refund reservations and eligible obligation. No cross-merchant reallocation. PromotionalGrant/Reservation/Redemption/Restoration separate restricted discount source, expiry and budget; CashPayable is informational only. RefundCase has original tender, verified refund status, exception approval and alternate method evidence if required. PayoutBatch maps eligible obligations to connected-account payout IDs and reconciled status; future multi-currency isolated by corridor, no launch FX. 
Receipt protected original, uploader, review and extraction; internal price observations. Handover report actual delivery, report timestamp, evidence, reminder, response, confirmation mode, confirmation_deadline and separate dispute_deadline. Dispute evidence and authorized resolution. Review stars/text with reveal rule. Referral immutable attribution, registration grant, qualifying order/cap. Bookmark/collective demand future. Event version/opaque IDs/UTC/environment/correlation; sanitized aggregate export.

## State boundaries
Request draft → pending_review → open → reserved → closed; expiry/cancellation; traveller cancellation releases reservation and reopens eligible request with historical order preserved. Offer submitted→accepted/rejected/withdrawn/expired; atomic reservation. Order awaiting_funding → funded → purchased → handover_pending → delivery_reported → receipt_confirmed or auto_confirmed → completed. Sequential: eligible actual report → immediate prompt, day 3/6 reminders and day 7 eligible silent auto-confirm. Recorded manual or auto confirmation starts separate 7-day dispute clock; early disputes allowed, no early release. No-show is not delivery. Payment/settlement/dispute states orthogonal: pending/confirmed/refunded/failed; not_eligible/eligible/payout_reserved/payout_pending/paid/failed/reversed; none/open/investigating/resolved. Provider events authoritative. Transactions serialize dispute/release, reopen/reservation, credit grant/cap. No penalty until valid traveller purchase receipt submission, then 20% gross inclusive fee, compensation remainder, refund 80% proportionately by tender subject to law. Receipt/cancel race serialized; no retroactive penalty. Monetary refund to original tender; eligible promo restoration only. On traveller cancellation refund original payment and reopen eligible request unfunded; fresh charge for new agreement, old refund remains tracked to completion. Account restrictions/disconnect cannot erase obligations. Serialize refunds and payout reservations per connected account; exclude all ineligible obligations and pending refunds from payout capacity. Weekly eligible cash only. Atomic grant reserve/redeem, reconcile liabilities and platform promo backing, no negative grants or duplicate refunds/assignment. Financial paths gated D-007–010/013/014/015.
