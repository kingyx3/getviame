# Logical domain model v0.2 (not physical schema)

User: Google subject/issuer, locale, indicative display currency, both roles, eligibility; provider KYC separate. Product parent → Variant → RegionalListing; image, brand, attributes, region/exclusivity, retailer, provenance. PriceObservation (variant, region, currency, amount, source user/public/receipt, time, tax basis, confidence, verification, visibility); unknown MSRP distinct from zero; private receipt-derived observations not public. 
Request: shopper, variant snapshot, source/destination, quantity, SGD total >= 1000 minor units, deadline/window, conditional food expiry, moderation. Trip: legs, origin/destination, dates, optional city/airport, visibility, no capacity. Offer: traveller, request, optional trip, dates, total SGD, expiry and terms version; multiple counteroffers, one active accepted order, historical cancelled orders retained. No self trade or partial quantity in MVP.
Order: immutable accepted snapshot, price/fee/currency, parties, quantity, acknowledged terms; revisions by consent recorded separately. Traveller bears costs. PaymentAttempt/ProviderEvent/Transfer/Refund/Payout: IDs, amount, currency, dedupe, authoritative status. Immutable balanced journal per currency for platform provider balance, reserved obligations and payable earnings; no customer cash wallet without clearance. PromotionalCredit separate (grant, redemption, expiry, reversal); future multi-currency isolated by corridor, no launch FX. 
Receipt protected original, uploader, review and extraction; internal price observations. Handover report actual delivery, report timestamp, evidence, reminder, response, confirmation mode and deadline. Dispute evidence and authorized resolution. Review stars/text with reveal rule. Referral immutable attribution, registration grant, qualifying order/cap. Bookmark/collective demand future. Event version/opaque IDs/UTC/environment/correlation; sanitized aggregate export.

## State boundaries
Request draft → pending_review → open → reserved → closed; expiry/cancellation; traveller cancellation releases reservation and reopens eligible request with historical order preserved. Offer submitted→accepted/rejected/withdrawn/expired; atomic reservation. Order awaiting_funding → funded → purchased → handover_pending → delivery_reported → receipt_confirmed or auto_confirmed → completed. Proposed concurrent 7-day clock from eligible actual delivery report; confirm early but no early release, auto-confirm only after silent deadline absent dispute/evidence exception. No-show is not delivery. Payment/settlement/dispute states orthogonal: pending/confirmed/refunded/failed; not_eligible/eligible/transfer_pending/transferred/payout_pending/paid/failed/reversed; none/open/investigating/resolved. Provider events authoritative. Transactions serialize dispute/release, reopen/reservation, credit grant/cap. Refund and new funding on reopened job proposed, no double money or silent reassignment. Exact clock, penalties and financial paths gated D-007–010/013/015.
