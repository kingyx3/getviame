Adopt useful workflows, not historical legal or fee assumptions. https://www.digitalnewsasia.com/airfrov-connects-buyers-travellers-lands-seed-funding ; closure linked to COVID travel disruption: https://mothership.sg/2021/03/airfrov-close/amp/
- MAS directory lists ShopBack Financial Services Singapore Pte. Ltd. as Major Payment Institution for account issuance, domestic money transfer and merchant acquisition: https://eservices.mas.gov.sg/fid/institution/detail/430460-SHOPBACK-FINANCIAL-SERVICES-SINGAPORE-PTE-LTD . This is not a general wallet exemption for GetViaMe. MAS explains limited-purpose loyalty treatment: https://ask.gov.sg/mas/questions/clx8ktis400bzryozdeghrztg ; Payment Services Act https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/PSA2019?ProvIds=Sc1-&ViewType=Within . Cash redemption, transferring/spending earnings or customer stored value require fact-specific counsel/provider assessment before design approval. Separate promotional credits from payout balances.
- Stripe PayNow supports Connect, SGD domestic payment, immediate confirmation, no manual capture, refund submission within 90 days, asynchronous refunds and no standard PayNow chargeback: https://docs.stripe.com/payments/paynow . Platform dispute process remains essential. Separate charges/transfers is a candidate not confirmed model: https://docs.stripe.com/connect/separate-charges-and-transfers . Stripe explicitly does not offer escrow; manual payout holding limits depend on account country: https://docs.stripe.com/connect/manual-payouts . Confirm provider approval, charge type/MoR, account countries, KYC, transfer/payout timing, refund liability, max lead time and settlement economics before real funds. PayNow collection does not imply PayNow payouts or cross-border account eligibility.
- Customs: Singapore personal GST relief excludes goods for trade/commercial use and goods carried for others; declare and account for duties/GST, permits as applicable: https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/goods-services-tax-%28gst%29/consumers/travellers-bringing-goods-into-singapore ; https://www.customs.gov.sg/at-customs/arriving-in-singapore/duty-free-concession-gst-relief . No assumption crew/passenger exemptions apply.
- Singapore Food Agency personal traveller imports are for private consumption only; commercial imports require the applicable licensing/registration and controls: https://www.sfa.gov.sg/bringing-food-for-private-consumption-from-overseas/general-rule-to-bring-food-into-singapore-non-commercial ; https://www.sfa.gov.sg/food-import-export/commercial-imports/what-you-need-to-know-for-import-of-food . Food is disabled for live marketplace until jurisdiction/category legal and operational gates clear. Prohibited/controlled items blocked pending permission and policy review.
- Treat all above as research, not legal clearance. Before launch verify latest laws, terms and provider support with qualified advisors and provider. Consumer rights, data protection, tax/MoR, airline employment/transport restrictions, insurance and destination/source-specific controls remain diligence items.

## Historical v0.3 refund and wallet diligence (superseded for current scope) (2026-11-26)
Stripe original processing/Connect/FX fees not returned: https://stripe.com/en-sg/pricing ; https://support.stripe.com/questions/understanding-fees-for-refunded-payments . PayNow supports original-method refunds, not a Stripe refund API into GetViaMe credits: https://support.stripe.com/questions/paynow-faq ; https://docs.stripe.com/payments/paynow . Voluntary credit election is a separate liability/settlement, not an executed Stripe refund; it may avoid new collection fees on the credited portion of future purchases, not recover sunk fees, and payout/transfer fees may remain. Connect terms restrict retention of transaction funds to transaction-related purposes, not indefinite wallet use: https://stripe.com/legal/ssa-service-terms . Obtain explicit provider and Singapore counsel clearance for per-user wallets, refund-funded and earned credits, safeguarding, KYC/AML, consumer refund rights and cash redemption.
