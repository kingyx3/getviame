# GetViaMe product brief v0.12 — comprehensive reconciliation draft

Owner direction through owner-answers-v0.12.md; later explicit answers supersede earlier evidence. Q127–205 approved for specification reconciliation; external clearance and revision review still required. Not authorization to implement or launch.

## Vision and priorities
Cross-border traveller-powered marketplace; Singapore-based frequent travellers, air crew and round trips returning to Singapore are initial focus, with global sourcing and future global corridor support. Priorities: overseas product accessibility > discovery > traveller supplementary income > price savings.

## Confirmed MVP
Installable responsive PWA, Google-only auth, age 18+, dual roles per account, payment-provider KYC for travellers. Singapore destination, SGD contracts/payment/transfer/payout only, no Stripe FX. Configurable legally permitted categories; food disabled until clearance. Permanent searchable parent-product/variant catalogue; user-confirmed enrichment from images, URLs, permitted APIs and AI, with sparse data supported. Shopper chooses price and delivery deadline; one variant/full quantity per request, minimum total S$10, individual units may be cheaper. MSRP and earnings indicative only, optional and never price constraints. Progressively learn from shopper inputs, public references and private receipts.
Traveller freely browses or enters origin/destination/dates (optional city/airport); no capacity input. Group similar jobs but keep orders separate. Multiple offers/counteroffers; shopper chooses. Traveller bears all lawful import GST, duties and purchasing costs, acknowledges terms before acceptance, retains savings; reprice/substitute/reduce quantity only with bilateral consent and verified incremental funding or original-tender refund; preserve original 30-day limit.
Inclusive fee max(S$1, 9% of gross rounded half-up in cents); initial configurable S$500 maximum with reviewed exceptions. Stripe Connect direct charges selected for MVP design, Stripe-managed pricing/risk preferred subject to eligibility and mandatory acceptance gates; PayNow-only MVP; confirmed provider funding (and approved platform-funded promotional coverage, if enabled) establishes acceptance before traveller spends. Traveller fronts retail cost. No customer-money wallets, top-ups, P2P, earnings credits or bonus. Weekly eligible cash payouts only; GetViaMe absorbs ordinary processor costs and cancellation losses from its own reserve. Provider/counsel clearance and technical acceptance gates required: exclusive payout control, platform-initiated refunds without merchant cooperation, independently funded shortfalls, preserved refund rights and account lifecycle safeguards. See direct-charge-acceptance-v0.11.md. Card authorisation with capture on valid purchase receipt and PayLah deferred. 
Meetup; traveller reports actual delivery, shopper confirms, reminders and auto-confirm after 7 days of silence. Sequential: manual or eligible auto-confirmation starts a separate seven-day dispute period; no early settlement. Notify immediately and remind on days 3/6; auto-confirm day 7 if eligible/silent. Acceptance to delivery ≤30 days; advance requests allowed. AI recommends dispute resolutions, humans approve financial decisions. No shopper cancellation penalty until eligible traveller purchase receipt submission (distinct from delivery confirmation); then 20% of gross inclusive of platform fee, balance to traveller, 80% refund proportionately by tender, disclosed to both. Full original-method monetary refund before receipt or on traveller cancellation; restore eligible promotional discount separately. Traveller cancellation tracks rate; full applicable refund and eligible request reopens unfunded; new offer requires fresh verified payment. Funded rematching deferred; no cross-merchant reuse of prior payment. Never release on unresolved dispute; exceptions require human review.
Mandatory private receipts with reviewed exception; uploader/staff access, price intelligence private. Verified star/text reviews blind until both submit or 14-day deadline; reputation indicators and in-app messaging.
Ranked country featured products, admin override; I want this too clones independent requests first, bookmarks and collective purchasing eventually. Platform-funded S$10 referee registration discount; S$10 referrer pending reward upon first qualifying funded order >=S$50 with some actual customer-origin payment, unlock only after successful completion and dispute clearance, cap 15; 180-day expiry per issued grant. Redemption on gross >=S$50, stacking up to full order; approved platform cash funding required. Committed face-value budget S$500 increments, pause new admissions at cap while honouring existing promises; human approval for next tranche. Referral off by default, no cash redemption. Manual trips first, opt-in travel planner integration and recurrent orders later, not MVP.

## Reconciled marketplace and pilot requirements
Guest browse non-sensitive public listings; authentication for actions; one account per person, risk-based shopper checks and required traveller KYC/SG lawful eligibility. Occasional individual sellers initially; professional sellers separately reviewed. Suspension/deletion blocks new activity but preserves obligations and lawful data retention. Unlisted categories held for human-approved classification; food/high-risk items off until cleared; compact permitted goods and beauty/skincare subject to eligibility, concentrated acquisition on selected approved corridors. Public catalogue corrections/merges reviewed; immutable snapshots. Material edits invalidate offers; default 48h offer validity bounded by request; owner selection/reservation not acceptance until funding. Competing offers inactive after funding, reconfirm on eligible reopening. Trip edits do not cancel funded obligations.
Valid receipt matches funded order, purchase date, merchant, product and quantity; allocate shared receipt lines, flag uncertainty, notify shopper; no retrospective penalty from late evidence. Authenticity and condition required. Voluntary transaction-specific handover code or manual confirmation starts separate dispute clock; silence only with sufficient actual evidence; mutually agreed reschedule/extension within original 30-day cap. Either party can dispute from funding; human review, one evidence-based appeal and statutory rights preserved. Ordinary cancellation after physical delivery is dispute handling. Post-receipt cancellation compensation after notice + separate 7-day challenge and weekly eligible payout; traveller retains goods subject to law. Risk-based restrictions for repeated unjustified cancellations by either role. Five eligible orders for rate display; comprehensive role-scoped transaction history, safe messaging and reporting.
Promotions earliest-expiry-first, 180 days from grant issuance (pending referrer issued at unlock); restoration grace for grants expiring during reservation, no automatic tranche recycling. Risk-based financial exposure limits are not luggage capacity; no early/instant payout; stress-tested reserve approved separately. AI-assisted support, authorised humans for exceptions. Controlled pilot and expansion only after demonstrated reliability, contribution margin and support readiness. Track approved payment/AI/marketing budgets separately without double counting infrastructure. Detailed requirements in active OpenSpec; no live approval.
