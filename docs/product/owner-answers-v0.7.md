- Q116: provider-neutral, optimise for minimum provider count, complete functional coverage and total cost, not Stripe loyalty. Analyse HitPay, Stripe and alternatives.
- Q119 B: absorb ordinary payment cancellation losses, track separately and maintain platform-funded risk reserve.
- Q120 C: remove refund wallets if they introduce additional regulatory complexity. Recommendation: exclude customer-funded stored value/refund wallets from MVP, use original-tender refunds, preserve platform-funded promotional credits separately subject to legal approval. Supersedes compulsory refund-credit intent and licensed shopper-wallet dependency; no traveller earnings credits, top-ups, P2P or general cash balance. Q117 payment method mix not explicitly answered.
- Funded rematching supported in principle. Explore card authorisation at offer acceptance, extension/re-authorisation as permitted and capture only on outcome. This challenges previous paid-acceptance/funded-before-purchase requirement; exact point of binding acceptance and capture remains a founder decision. No automatic off-session reauthorisation assumption; no traveller purchase on unsecured payment without approved risk allocation.

## Cost and lifecycle recommendation (proposal)
- RFQ Airwallex and Stripe in parallel as leading single-provider options; HitPay only if expressly supports non-business individuals, conditional settlement, payout and original-tender refund across required period. Benchmark Omise and DBS direct bespoke arrangement; no multi-provider MVP absent quantified saving and compliant integrated settlement. Compare all fees, monthly active individual sellers, weekly batching, GST, reserves, chargebacks, partial capture, authorisation/void/renewal, payout, failed attempts, onboarding and engineering/ops costs. Use current public retail rates for illustration only.
- Example SGD100 PayNow HitPay 0.95 vs Stripe 1.30; at SGD10 HitPay 0.20 vs Stripe 0.13. SGD50 HitPay 0.45 vs Stripe 0.65; SGD500 HitPay 3.55 vs Stripe 6.50. 100 SGD100 accepted orders, 20 early cancellations: HitPay PayNow collection SGD95 vs hypothetical zero-fee void cards (80*3.30=264); break-even cancellation fraction 1-0.95/3.30=71.2%, ignoring ancillary fees. Stripe PayNow 130 vs cards 312, break-even 66.7%. Card hold reduces sunk fee on void but not necessarily total cost. Stripe $2/active seller + payout 0.25%+0.50 can dominate small low-frequency jobs; if one SGD100 successful job per seller per month, illustrative 1.30+2+0.73=4.03, before taxes; 9 gross fee less 4.03 =4.97. Seek actual platform quotes.
- Recommended PayNow captured at confirmed acceptance, provider-held per order until confirmed delivery + separate seven-day dispute period, cash payouts weekly, full original-tender refund before valid purchase receipt or traveller cancellation and 80% after valid purchase receipt (9% fee minimum SGD1 inside 20% penalty). Promo portion restored as eligible restricted grant, never cash. Funded rematch only with explicit consent, same request, time-limited, provider-authorised; if no replacement/consent expires refund promptly; new agreement and price adjustment consent, no double assignment. No generic balance.
- Card optional feature flag: distinguish offer selected/authorised from captured/funded. Use short auth for purchase readiness or short rematch window; capture successfully before traveller spends money; no guaranteed hold renewal. Full void before capture on cancellation; captured funds refund under normal rules; capture before provider deadline with consent or ask for new auth; no purchases on expired/uncertain auth. Outcome-only capture after delivery or dispute is not reliable for 30+14 days, and risks traveller purchase costs if renewed authorisation fails. If founder chooses auth-only binding acceptance or capture after purchase, require explicit policy for loss liability, charge failure, expiry/reauth and receipt race; provider/counsel/network approval. Capture at purchase readiness rather than after delivery is recommended.
- Use original tender and provider-confirmed payment/void/refund/payout statuses, not UI success. Keep customer funds provider-side; no pooled business bank account unless specific approved safeguarded arrangement. No open dispute release; handle late refunds past native limit via approved exception. Order-linked hold is not cash wallet. Keep immutable transaction history, reconcile all fees and exceptions, no disabling financial controls.
- Ring-fence platform-funded cancellation loss reserve, track refunded/voided costs and rematch savings, no refusal of legally owed refund when reserve depleted. No new customer fees approved.
