## Rapyd primary-source checks
- MAS directory confirms Rapyd Holdings Pte Ltd MPI with account issuance, e-money issuance, domestic/cross-border transfer and merchant acquisition: https://eservices.mas.gov.sg/fid/institution/detail/227554-RAPYD-HOLDINGS-PTE-LTD .
- Singapore PayNow QR docs explicitly specify SGD, completed-payment webhook, no standard disputes/chargebacks, and **30-day refund limit from original transaction**: https://docs.rapyd.net/en/paynow-qr.html . This conflicts with up to 30-day delivery + separate 7+7-day confirmation/dispute and extensions; cannot rely on native PayNow refund API after day 30. Obtain authorised late-refund/redemption mechanism, even if ordinary refunds become wallet credits.
- Escrow API supports split/partial release and auto-release 1–90 days: https://docs.rapyd.net/en/request-a-payment-into-escrow.html ; https://docs.rapyd.net/en/releasing-funds-from-escrow.html . Automatic release may violate open-dispute rule; confirm provider-side non-expiring hold or extension mechanism, including after maximum; do not shorten customer rights. Cancel escrow releases to sellers, NOT buyer refund: https://docs.rapyd.net/en/canceling-an-escrow.html . No production reliance until tested/approved.
- Personal wallets and wallet transfer APIs documented: https://docs.rapyd.net/en/creating-a-rapyd-wallet.html ; https://docs.rapyd.net/en/transfer-funds-between-wallets.html . However current published Singapore end-user terms clauses 7.2 and 9.1 restrict use to business/commercial purposes, not personal/household transactions; 8.5 allows withdrawal, 8.6 inactivity fees and 8.7 closure/redemption: https://www.rapyd.net/security-compliance/rapyd-terms-singapore/ . Critical blocker: obtain applicable retail consumer terms and confirm no inappropriate principal expiry/fees. General API availability not proof of Singapore shopper wallet eligibility.
- SG Collect terms clause 17 restrict ordinary refunds to original payment method, permit additional refund fees and require merchant to fund shortfalls: https://www.rapyd.net/security-compliance/rapyd-collect-sg/ . Obtain written approval for separate licensed wallet reallocation/refund workflow; no disguised refund or double payout. Confirm fees refunded on cancellation, refunds/escrow/wallet transfers/withdrawals, setup/monthly minimums, reserves, KYC, payout costs.
- SGD bank payout API examples and SG terms: https://docs.rapyd.net/en/create-payout.html ; https://www.rapyd.net/security-compliance/rapyd-disburse-sg/ . Confirm individual traveller onboarding and weekly batching, no FX and acceptable reserve requirements.
- Card authorisation/capture may avoid capture fees on early cancellation if priced accordingly; ordinary authorisation expiry is documented at seven days (preauth permissions vary): https://docs.rapyd.net/en/capture-payment.html ; https://docs.rapyd.net/en/offering-key-terms.html . Do not replace confirmed payment requirement with authorisation only without founder approval. PayNow is immediate bank transfer, not card preauthorisation.
- Public pricing: https://www.rapyd.net/products/pricing/ ; no binding GetViaMe quote or confirmed original fee treatment. No provider contact, sandbox, legal opinion or licence exemption verified. Earlier v0.5 provider research must be read subject to these newly identified blockers.

## Proposed cancellation-cost mitigation (not yet approved)
1. Prefer low all-in cost SGD PayNow if contracted; compare fixed fees, refunds, wallet charges, holding/withdrawal fees and merchant reserves; negotiate original-fee return or waiver, zero/low refund fees, no/low monthly minimums.
2. Where traveller cancels and shopper still wants same product, offer consent-based order-specific rematch using existing provider-held funds (not a general wallet) if provider/legal model permits. Keep historical cancelled order and new acceptance separate; explicit price difference approval and top-up/return; if shopper declines or no match within approved short deadline, promptly refund through approved path. Do not bind replacement or extend without consent.
3. For general refunds, provider-issued shopper wallet with optional/approved credit retention and lawful withdrawals; only if total cost beats refund and re-collection. Never spend customer principal on platform costs. No forced wallet enrollment or withheld mandatory refund.
4. Ring-fence platform-funded cancellation loss reserve, independent of customer money, promo commitments and traveller obligations; record actual unrecovered collection/refund fees per cancelled order, segmented by initiator/reason/payment method; monitor loss per completed order and loss budget, review abnormal actors, throttle new risky commitments only, always process owed refunds. Propose alerts and limits using actual quote/pilot data; no automatic budget or fee changes.
5. Retain existing no-penalty before receipt and 20% after eligible traveller purchase receipt. No new admin fee or pass-through without founder approval, provider/network and consumer-law review; never charge shopper for traveller fault under proposed default. No unauthorised security deposits or wallet top-ups.
6. Illustrative, not vendor pricing: SGD 100 collection cost 1.00, refund fee 0.50 -> early original-method cancellation costs SGD 1.50; wallet reallocation assumed 0.10 -> immediate cost 1.10, future collection cost potentially avoided, but withdrawal costs/fees/overhead may eliminate savings. 100 SGD 100 orders, 20 early full refunds, 80 completed: gross fees 720, collection 100, refund 10, remaining 610 before payouts, other costs, tax and incentives. Reserve sizing from stress exposure and actual fee schedule, separate from SGD 500 referral allocation.
