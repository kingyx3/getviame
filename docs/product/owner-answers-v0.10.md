## Verified public documentation (2026-11-28)
- Platform can initiate full or partial refund of its own direct charge using platform secret key with Stripe-Account header and charge ID; connected-account approval per refund not required while authorized. Application fee is not automatically refunded; refund_application_fee=true refunds fee in full/proportionately, false allows separate fee refund. https://docs.stripe.com/connect/direct-charges.md?platform=web&ui=stripe-hosted
- Refund is debited to traveller account; general Connect charge guide describes insufficient balance as pending, but payment-method refund guide explicitly says non-card refunds fail when available funds insufficient. For PayNow do not rely on pending or automatic retry; verify actual API behavior, balance before refund and authoritative events; reconcile before any retry. https://docs.stripe.com/connect/charges ; https://docs.stripe.com/refunds ; https://docs.stripe.com/payments/paynow . PayNow full/partial asynchronous, 90-day native window, T+1 availability, no card-style chargeback; failed/expired refund approved alternate return to verified payer.
- Stripe standard fee billing to account (v1) or stripe (v2) incurs no additional standard Connect fees. https://docs.stripe.com/connect/direct-charges-fee-payer-behavior . Managed Risk covers unrecoverable connected-account negatives if eligible, not guaranteed customer refund funding or platform obligations. https://docs.stripe.com/connect/risk-management/managed-risk .
- Manual payouts expressly supported for delivery/refund scenarios; 90 days Singapore, not escrow. https://docs.stripe.com/connect/manual-payouts . Full Dashboard platform controls restrict schedule but user can still manually pay out unless additional restriction enabled; removal of connected account resets platform controls. https://docs.stripe.com/connect/platform-controls-for-stripe-dashboard-accounts . Need dedicated accounts, exclusive payout control across dashboard/API/off-platform and bank account changes, disconnection/closure handling; verify before onboarding live payments. Embedded-only Stripe-managed risk dashboard alternative to assess (required account management/notification components); no reliance on preview. Do not promise blanket merchant account freeze or arbitrary bank debit. Stripe Account Debits require platform loss liability and Singapore is not listed. https://docs.stripe.com/connect/account-debits .
- Direct-charge merchant of record is traveller; disclose clearly and ensure actual sale/service contracts reflect this, including legal authority for platform refunds and dispute adjudication. https://docs.stripe.com/connect/merchant-of-record . No license exemption implied.
