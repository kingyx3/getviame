# Founder decision v0.9 — Stripe Connect selection (2026-11-28)

## Approval record
Founder explicitly directed: "ok, go with stripe connect please" following the PayNow versus deferred card capture cost comparison. Stripe Connect is the selected single payment provider for GetViaMe's MVP design and provider onboarding/diligence, superseding the provisional Airwallex ranking in payment-provider-diligence-v0.8.md and the earlier provider-neutral selection process (D-010 / Q116 / Q126). Approval scope is vendor selection, not a specific Connect charge type, fee contract, paid service commitment, implementation, live funds, regulatory clearance or production deployment. Base main 7e26b982e2c199e883744545b0e99da390254612; previous draft head 99327239a9da23ffb55d637a95fded8e6e138050; draft PR #2.

## Design direction
- PayNow-first SGD checkout is the recommended MVP baseline following the founder's comparison and selection; deferred card authorisation/capture at valid purchase receipt remains future scope (Q123), and direct PayLah is optional future scope. Q122 payment method exclusivity and Q124 future reauthorisation policy have not been separately explicitly approved; do not enable other methods by default.
- No customer-money wallets or earnings wallets; original-method monetary refunds; provider-approved request-specific funded rematching until request expiry subject to retention/refund limits; restricted platform-funded promotions only. Preserve all v0.8 terms, 9% inclusive fee min SGD1, SGD10 min order, receipt-triggered 20% cancellation penalty, weekly eligible cash settlement, platform-funded ordinary cancellation loss reserve.
- Evaluate Stripe Connect separate charges and transfers first because delayed recipient transfer and fee allocation are documented; do not assume it is approved or legally appropriate. Compare other Connect charge types/account configurations for custody, liability, exact seller net and fees. No customer funds in GetViaMe's ordinary bank account or unrestricted internal wallet. No escrow claim. Retain narrow payment adapter boundaries, but do not build parallel providers for MVP.
- Illustrative rationale using previously recorded standard pricing (not a quote): SGD100 PayNow 1.30 versus domestic card 3.90; assuming no uncaptured authorisation fees, break-even pre-capture cancellation rate = 1 - 1.30/3.90 = 66.67%. With immediate card capture, no such savings. Connect active-account/payout fees remain material, particularly for small occasional traveller orders. No approval to alter minimum order or charge fees to users.
