## Accounting and operational proposals requiring final policy review
- Normal fee F=max(100, half-up(0.09*gross_cents)); traveller gross-F. Post-purchase-receipt shopper cancellation P=half-up(0.20*gross_cents); platform F, traveller P-F, shopper gross-P source-specific credit restoration. SGD 100 -> 9/11/80; SGD 10 -> 1/1/8. Pre-receipt full credit refund and zero traveller compensation; original processing costs borne by platform proposed. GST and processor costs remain open. Proportionate mixed tender rounding must sum exactly. Disputed/invalid/late receipt cannot create automatic penalty; record effective timestamps atomically. Purchase notification independent of delivery confirmation.
- 100% promotional tender requires platform-funded cash settlement (SGD 50 referral-only order -> SGD 45.50 traveller cash payout before other costs); no unfunded payouts. Wallet refund principal is customer money, not marketing expense; promotional liabilities and reserve tracked separately. Fully promotional orders may trigger circular referral farming; recommend at least some real customer-origin tender to qualify referrer, not for redemption. This is an open interpretation, not founder approval.
- Proposed grant event on verified funded acceptance; show reward pending/non-spendable until cancellation/dispute/fraud risk clears, or clawback model if immediate spendable required. Founder requested simpler paid-order qualification; confirm timing/availability and canceled-order consequences. Never confiscate legitimate unrelated principal or create negative customer balance to claw back promo.
- Proposed initial SGD 500 cumulative committed promotional face-value budget (not monthly or merely redeemed amount), including both SGD 10 promises per admitted referee, so at most 25 fully reserved pairs at first tranche. Admission/reservation atomic; referrer cap and expiry honored, existing promised rewards survive pause. Admin explicitly approves each next SGD 500 tranche, no autonomous expansion; clarify if budget should instead be measured on earned grants or net actual spend. Referral flag off by default.
- Late funding after 15-minute checkout expiration cannot bind a second order; validate ownership and idempotency, reconcile and return funds through approved process. Provider session/QR expiry may differ from application reservation; verify cancellation semantics.
