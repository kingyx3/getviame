## Confirmed direction
- Q121 A: Remove shopper refund wallets entirely. Return monetary refunds to original payment method through approved provider; retain only separately accounted platform-funded promotional credits, subject to clearance. No customer top-ups, peer transfers, earnings wallets or generic stored-value balance. This supersedes prior compulsory credit-refund and licensed wallet-partner requirements. Prior accepted proportional mixed-tender cancellation policy now returns monetary portion to original method and restores applicable promotional portion to its restricted grant; never cash out promotional value.
- Q123: Deferred card authorisation/capture is not required at present; future direction is authorise at offer selection and capture upon traveller submission of purchase receipt (when traveller buys). This is a future gated capability, not current MVP. It overrides the earlier proposed capture-before-purchase timing for that future feature. Resolve the residual risk of purchase occurring before successful capture before enabling. Current funded acceptance / payment confirmed before purchase remains unchanged.
- Q125 D: Consent-based funded rematching can continue until original shopper request expires, rather than fixed 72 hours. This is a maximum business duration, not authorisation to hold funds beyond provider/legal limits. Preserve existing shopper cancellation/refund rights; never hold money indefinitely or create general-purpose balances. Same request only; new offer explicitly accepted, price adjustment consent, separate order history and no double assignment.
- Q122, Q124 and Q126 not explicitly answered in this turn. Earlier Q116 provider-neutral cost/coverage/minimum-provider direction remains. Proposed defaults: PayNow-first MVP, cards/PayLah later behind gates; customer reauthorisation as required under provider rules (future); lowest total-cost compliant single provider preferred. Do not infer new approvals from omission.

## Proposed safeguards to review
- Rematching ends at earliest of original request expiry, shopper withdrawal of consent/refund request, successful replacement acceptance, or provider/legal permitted funds-retention deadline. If retention must end before original request expiry, promptly refund and allow request to remain open unfunded; fresh payment required for a replacement. No reset of original collection/refund clock merely because replacement traveller is selected. Recheck 30-day maximum acceptance-to-delivery and sequential 7+7-day confirmation/dispute horizon against remaining provider hold/refund limits on every replacement. Preserve legal exception refunds after native API window. Rematch duration can exceed 30 days in advance requests, so do not promise provider retention until arbitrary request expiry.
- Future receipt-triggered card capture: distinguish authorised, capture-pending, captured, expired/failed, voided, refunded. Check provider-authoritative capture deadline and sufficient valid authorised amount immediately before purchase; receipt submission invokes idempotent capture, reconciles provider outcome, notifies shopper; expired/failed capture after purchase enters exception review. Valid authorisation reduces but does not eliminate capture failure. Explicit loss allocation, receipt/cancel race handling, cancellation compensation, reauthorisation consent and provider approval required before this feature can launch. No automatic indefinite authorisation extensions. Keep feature off and do not build speculative card flow in MVP.
- Current MVP payment acceptance requires confirmed funding; normal completed fee 9% inclusive, min SGD1, min SGD10 job; before eligible traveller purchase receipt full original-tender refund on shopper cancellation, after valid receipt 20% inclusive penalty/80% refund subject to approved rules; traveller cancellation full refund or consent-based rematch. Weekly cash payouts after separate dispute window; platform absorbs ordinary cancellation costs with separate reserve. All subject to provider and legal clearance.
