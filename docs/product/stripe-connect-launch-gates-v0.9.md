## Verified public capability and limitations
- MAS FID lists Stripe Payments Singapore Pte Ltd as MPI for account issuance, domestic/cross-border transfer, merchant acquisition and e-money issuance: https://eservices.mas.gov.sg/fid/institution/detail/219107-STRIPE-PAYMENTS-SINGAPORE-PTE-LTD . Stripe SG disclosure states relevant money safeguarded in pooled trust accounts with Singapore banks; default payout speed T+7 calendar days. Identify whose relevant money and contractual entitlement; not proof of shopper trust or per-order isolation: https://stripe.com/en-sg/legal/singapore-disclosures .
- Stripe manual payout docs specify Singapore within general 90-day connected-account payout window, not permission for arbitrary pre-transfer hold in platform account. Stripe is not escrow. https://docs.stripe.com/connect/manual-payouts . Ordinary order can need 30+7+7=44 days to eligibility, up to 7 more to next weekly batch plus bank processing; unresolved disputes/rematch can exceed either limit.
- Critical finding: Stripe's funds segregation private preview currently lists BE CH DE DK ES FR GB NL SE US only and Visa/Mastercard/Discover/Amex/Swish only, NOT Singapore or PayNow. Ordinary source_transaction/transfer_group links payments/transfers but does NOT isolate balance or protect funds from unrelated debits. https://docs.stripe.com/connect/funds-segregation ; https://docs.stripe.com/connect/separate-charges-and-transfers.md?platform=web&integration=checkout&ui=stripe-hosted . Do not rely on preview or market as escrow.
- PayNow all Connect charge types documented; immediate payment confirmation, SGD only, minimum SGD0.50, no authorisation/manual capture/off-session; 90-day original-payment refund submission window, full/partial asynchronous, typically up to five minutes, cannot cancel refund. Failed refund returns funds to Stripe balance and requires alternate method. No standard PayNow chargeback; platform dispute obligations remain. https://docs.stripe.com/payments/paynow . Check MCC/restricted goods, connected capability paynow_payments when applicable.
- Stripe services terms Payments section 5.3(b) expressly identifies advance sale without Stripe consent as a risk trigger; obtain express written consent for delayed delivery. Connect clauses 3.1/3.2/5 specify platform liabilities and certain Singapore connected-account receipt obligations; scope depends on agreement, risk model, account configuration. https://stripe.com/en-sg/legal/ssa-service-terms . No assumption Stripe assumes consumer/merchant obligations.
## Charge topology comparison — not approved
A. Separate charges and transfers (SCT): platform charge, fees and refund liability on platform balance; transfer after cleared dispute, recipient determined later; refund does NOT reverse associated transfers automatically, recovery may fail. Best rematch technical fit but platform account balance, settlement merchant and permitted retention are substantive regulatory/contractual blockers; no per-order isolation in standard SG PayNow. Do not rely on an internal journal as safeguarding.
B. Direct charge: connected account receives funds, seller business of record; conditional manual bank payout up to published limit if payout control available; Stripe documents configurable fee payer, but no promise of no-platform-fee pricing combined with full control. Reassigning seller of an existing direct charge is not supported by ordinary flow; rematch likely refund/recollect. Must verify seller account control, payout restrictions, refund liability, KYC, exact net, fee reimbursement and whether seller is correct contracting merchant.
C. Destination charge: platform charge with immediate transfer to connected-account balance; delayed bank payout distinct from preventing seller balance transfer. Platform fee/refund liability, transfer reversal can fail or be incomplete. Not first choice for funded rematch; only if actual legal/contractual structure and no access to ineligible funds are approved. https://docs.stripe.com/connect/charges . Do not treat destination charge or manual payout as equivalent to pre-transfer hold. No change in product requirements approved.
Recommendation: request written Stripe review of SCT for domestic PayNow, then direct-charge controlled payout alternative if compliant and founder approves rematch fallback. No implementation until actual structure cleared.

## Rematch/refund validation design
Original payment date retained per charge; compute approved retention and refund deadline per source. Before replacement, require remaining lifecycle (new delivery + confirmation + dispute + payout margin) fits provider/legal horizon. Shopper can withdraw consent and receive refund; if limit reached first, refund and leave request unfunded. No generic credit or deadline reset; no new seller transfer before eligible, explicit new agreement and price consent. For SCT, same-price reuse plausibly one original charge/one later transfer; lower price partial refund, higher collect delta (each charge linked with original dates); verify MoR/descriptor/recipient authorisation before enabling. Never simply reassign a seller-specific direct charge. If rematch not approved, propose refund/recollect to founder.
Refund API before day90 with authoritative status; pending not success; track cumulative principal to prevent over-refund; refund funding must cover sunk original fee. For deadline approaching, alerts and authorized escalation before cut-off; never release to seller just to satisfy deadline or auto-refund without lawful decision. Failed or >90-day required refund: verify original payer using provider-approved proof, only approved return method, maker-checker approval, record original charge/refund case/recipient proof and bank/provider confirmation, reserve liability until reconciled. Do not use ordinary Connect transfers to unverified shoppers or invent Stripe original-method capability beyond window; exact mechanism BLOCKED. Provider confirmation and legal approval required. No wallet.

## Acceptance matrix (design only; no Stripe test credentials or execution)
- A1 onboarding: eligible SG individual, actual transfer/payment capabilities and payout control; blocked incomplete KYC, no off-platform payout.
- A2 funding: SGD100 success/failed/duplicate/expired QR; only signed verified success binds; refund late duplicate; confirmed charge amount/currency/account; no transfer before release.
- A3 timeline: delivery day30, auto-confirm day37, dispute expiry day44, first weekly payout thereafter; early dispute, unresolved dispute approaching hold deadline, no premature release; provider confirms longer exceptions.
- A4 fee and cancellation: SGD10 normal fee1/seller9; SGD100 normal fee9/seller91; pre-receipt refund100, seller0; valid receipt penalty20, refund80/seller11/fee9; receipt/cancel race; insufficient platform balance/failed transfer; no refund fee charged to shopper.
- A5 rematch: same request, explicit consent, changed seller, same/90/110 SGD, delta collection or partial refund, opt-out, expiry, provider cutoff, original clock and no double assignment.
- A6 refunds: day40, day89, day90 boundary per provider semantics, rejected/failed/late refund, verify pending/success, correct alternate return; refund after any transfer must reconcile reversal/shortfall; replay cannot duplicate.
- A7 payout: several eligible orders same traveller one weekly payout; open dispute excluded, partial eligibility, bank failure/unknown result, reconcile transfer vs payout and actual fees.
- A8 promotion: SGD70 cash + SGD30 grant full refund restores sources; post-receipt proportionate allocation e.g. cash refund56/promo restoration24 (penalty14 cash +6 promo); full SGD50 promotional order seller45.50 from platform funds, no bogus charge; Stripe minimum cash SGD0.50, proposed explicit promo adjustment or fully promotional checkout if 1–49 cents remaining, not silent surcharge; no positive tender below provider minimum. Do not change qualifying threshold without approval.
- A9 replay and safety: signed webhooks, idempotency on timeout, reconcile unknown response, concurrent refund/release/rematch/receipt, no double charge/payout, false payment events rejected; actual fees + balances reconcile; live flags off by default.
Pass criteria: written provider and counsel evidence plus sandbox transaction IDs, logs, reconciled journals, actual tests and independent review. Sandbox cannot establish legal permission or real-time expiry behavior alone. No real funds or live account changes.
