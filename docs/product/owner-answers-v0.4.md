## Proposed interpretation and open decisions (not approvals)
- Normal fee F=max(100 cents, rounded 8% gross); cancelled after valid receipt: penalty P=rounded 20% gross, platform retains F, traveller P-F, refund gross-P. Example SGD 100: 8/12/80; SGD 10: 1/1/8. Clarify half-up rounding, GST, refund/processor costs and mixed tender. Before receipt platform retains no fee proposed; original processing cost sunk. Promotional amounts must never be converted to cash or unrestricted refund principal. Clarify penalties on promo-funded orders and funding shortfalls before allowing stacking.
- Mandatory cash-origin refund credits still represent customer funds; non-withdrawability alone does not establish limited-purpose exemption. No refund credit issuance, redemption or compulsory refund term until counsel and provider written approval; original-method refunds when required; founder to confirm fallback for cash-only pilot and whether all refund credits non-withdrawable. Sources: https://ask.gov.sg/mas/questions/clx8ktis400bzryozdeghrztg ; https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/PSA2019?ProvIds=Sc1-&ViewType=Within . No claim of licensing exemption.
- Payment-pending checkout reservation with timeout; no confirmed acceptance before authoritative provider success/atomic eligible credit funding. Late/duplicate success cannot double-assign request; reconcile and return funds under approved policy. Confirm reservation timeout and treatment of offer withdrawal while payment in flight.
- Referral programme disabled by default. On enablement, published terms/version, cohort eligibility, immutable attribution, earned grants honoured, no retroactive removal merely due to flag/budget. Proposed pause new referral enrolment (not existing promises) if risk or spending threshold reached; no unapproved spending. Confirm whether stacking permits 100% tender, how SGD 50 threshold applies, whether referee credit stacks and whether approved no-hard-cap campaign includes operational spend alerts.
- Features flags server-side, environment-scoped, audited changes, safe defaults, role-limited; agents may propose or operate only pre-approved low-risk nonprod scopes; production/financial/compliance enablement human-authorized. Disabling feature must preserve existing orders, accrued rewards, refunds, reconciliation and legally required workflows.
- Transaction history: shopper and traveller order/tender/fee/refund/credit grant/redemption/expiry/reversal/settlement timeline with amount, status, date, order and reference; admin reconciled immutable balanced ledger with append-only corrections, provider IDs, access/audit and source-specific lots. Private sourcing cost/receipt not exposed to shoppers. No unsupported financial statements.
- Beauty priority subject to applicable product classification, supply/notification and importer obligations; not all cosmetics automatically eligible. https://www.hsa.gov.sg/cosmetic-products/overview/ . Food remains off.
- Existing seven-day confirmation + separate seven-day dispute clock unchanged, no early release; code evidence does not waive dispute.
