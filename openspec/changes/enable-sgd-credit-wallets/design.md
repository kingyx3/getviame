# Design — Individual SGD credit wallets

Draft v0.3; see docs/product/wallet-policy.md, decisions.md, research.md and domain-model.md. User-visible wallet may combine totals, but immutable ledger separates refund principal, earned principal, bonus and referral credit, and cash payable is not spendable. Store source/order/tender and currency, restrictions and eligibility, consent, expiry by type, available/reserved/pending and settlement status. Integer cents and balanced journal; provider backing and settlement reconciled separately. Enforce atomic reserve/redeem, no negative balance, dedupe event/command, release on failed checkout, source-specific reversals and refund limits. If Firestore integrity not proven revisit database. No user funds held indefinitely in generic Stripe Connect balance.
Refund: original provider fee already incurred. On eligible cancellation, offer original-method refund and, only after provider/legal approval, informed credit alternative. No double refund or coercion, credit not Stripe refund. Preserve original tender, do not cash out promotions through refund. Reopen request separately; fresh acceptance, reserve allowed credit and collect only eligible remaining cash. Credit-only checkout and split tender require approved provider/legal settlement model. Retain customer principal without arbitrary expiry pending policy.
Traveller election: default scheduled cash; elective credits +2% from platform fee. Proposed gross basis: S$100 yields S$92 cash or S$92 earned principal plus S$2 bonus; S$6 gross platform fee remains before costs. Confirm base/election timing, reversibility and exclusions. Proposed election locked at eligibility, no credit before sequential dispute closes or double cash payout. Referral eligibility and expiry per growth spec; no principal treated as promotional expiry. Tests synthetic; obtain written signoff for issuer, safeguarding, KYC/AML, limits, refund/withdrawal rights, accounting and funding sources. No real-money enablement prior to approvals.
