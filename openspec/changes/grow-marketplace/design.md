# Design — Grow demand, discovery and referrals (v0.9 draft)
Rank featured products by demand/availability/conversion/trends with admin override. Clone request first; bookmarks and collective purchase later. Referrals OFF until approved provider and counsel funding/classification. S$10 referee eligible registration grant; S$10 referrer pending at verified first qualifying funded >=S$50 gross order with some actual customer payment, unlock after successful completion and dispute clearance, cap 15, 180 days from issuance per grant. Full stacking on gross >=S$50; no fixed cash co-pay for redemption; all-promo order cannot qualify referrer. Reserve S$20 promised face value per admitted pair conservatively in S$500 cumulative tranches; pause new enrolments, honour promised rewards, human approves tranche increases. No purchased or cash-origin credits. Use platform cash for discounted traveller obligations, never convert promo to cash; atomic grant reserve/redeem/restoration, no self-trade, deduped pending/grant/unlock notifications. Preserve grant provenance and expiry; exact restoration/rounding/minimum residual-payment handling to review. See wallet-policy.md and Stripe launch gates. No customer wallet.
