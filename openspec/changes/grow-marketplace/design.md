# Design — Grow demand, discovery and referrals

Draft, v0.2; see domain-model.md, architecture.md, agent-operations.md, metrics.md and decision register.

Rank featured products by demand/availability/conversion/trends with admin override. Clone request first; bookmarks and collective purchase later. S$10 spend-only referee credit at eligible registration, S$10 referrer after first qualifying completed transaction, cap five referees, expiry 180 days, configurable threshold. S$50 minimum order to redeem S$10 referral credit; proposed one grant per order; minimum co-pay/stacking, qualification threshold for referrer, attribution, fraud/reversals and legal classification pending. Atomic one grant per identity/trigger, no self referral, separate credit journal; success email only after grant.
