# Design — Complete funded marketplace transaction

Draft v0.3. SGD only, no Stripe FX. PayNow Connect subject to provider/counsel approval (charge type, MoR, KYC, liability, refund horizon, permitted retention, scheduled payouts), no escrow. Fund after acceptance before purchase; 30-day max acceptance-to-delivery, separate confirmation/dispute and exceptional resolution extend financial horizon. Signed events, balanced journal, outbox, idempotent settlement and reconciliation. Separate wallet change for individual SGD credit balances, optional credit refunds and bonus; no assumption Connect can issue wallets.
Mandatory private receipt with approved exceptions; uploader/staff access, extracted prices internal. Meetup actual delivery report and evidence, server time; immediate prompt, day 3/day 6 reminders, eligible silent auto-confirm day 7. Manual or auto confirmation starts separate 7×24h dispute clock; no early release. Early disputes allowed; no-show/contested evidence blocks auto-confirm and payout. Proposed UTC half-open deadlines; record actual durable confirmation, never backdate delayed job to shorten dispute rights. AI recommends, human approves exceptional finance.
No shopper cancellation penalty before eligible purchase receipt submission. After submission 20% payable to traveller; disclose both parties before commitment and at cancellation. Proposed base agreed total, no platform penalty share, law/invalid receipt/nonperformance exceptions; serialize receipt submission vs effective cancellation. Retain mutual consent/dispute process after purchase where applicable; clarify exact interaction and goods/return rights. Traveller cancellation records rate, reopens eligible job, original order immutable; proposed shopper choice original-method refund or approved credit refund, not forced. Fresh accepted order, reserve new tender, no silent reassignment or duplicate obligations. Scheduled cash payouts after eligibility; credit election separate. See wallet-policy.md and decisions.md.
