# Design — Complete funded marketplace transaction (v0.9 draft)
Read docs/product/stripe-connect-launch-gates-v0.9.md, wallet-policy.md, decisions.md and engineering/architecture.md. Single Stripe Connect, SGD PayNow-first, no FX. Charge topology must be approved; separate charges/transfers is a candidate only. Funds segregation not available for SG PayNow in published preview. No claims of escrow or customer wallets.
State: selected/awaiting_funding -> funded (provider confirmation + eligible funded promotion) -> purchased -> actual delivery report -> confirmed -> dispute clearance -> settlement eligible -> transferred -> payout pending -> paid. Payment, refund, cancellation, evidence and payout states orthogonal; immutable historical order and source allocation. Signed webhook, authoritative retrieve, transactional guards, balanced journal, deduped outbox with stable idempotency keys, reconcile unknown outcomes before retry, no premature acknowledgement. No duplicate funded assignment on checkout expiry/replay.
Receipt mandatory private with approved exception; uploader/staff access, price observations internal. Serialize receipt submission vs cancellation. 30-day acceptance-to-delivery max; report actual handover, immediate prompt/day3/day6 reminders, eligible silent auto-confirm day7, distinct confirmation +7 day dispute, early disputes allowed; no-show/contested evidence blocks, never backdate. AI advises; human approves exceptional finance.
Fee F=max(100, half-up(9% gross cents)); normal seller G-F. Before valid purchase receipt, refund all cash to original method and restore eligible promos; after valid receipt, penalty P=half-up(20% G), F fee, P-F seller, G-P refund allocated proportionately to original cash/promo sources. Exact rounding/expiry exception design pending; no promo cash conversion. Processor costs funded separately by platform. Explicit lawful cancellation disclosures, no penalty on invalid/late receipt. No early normal settlement; cancellation compensation requires cleared approved cancellation/dispute handling.
Rematch after traveller cancellation only with shopper opt-in, same request, new agreement and price consent, original payment allocation retained only within permitted deadlines; refund on opt-out/expiry or retention deadline, preserve order history; never reset clocks. Preflight new deadline + confirmation/dispute/weekly payout against original funding horizon. If unapproved, refund/recollect fallback needs founder approval. Under separate charges, delay seller transfer; source_transaction links charge, transfer_group aids reconciliation but does not isolate funds. Don't assume seller/MoR can change; Stripe/counsel signoff required. Refunding platform charge does not automatically reverse transfers. Late/failed refund approved verified-payer exception with maker/checker and no double refund; no automatic alternate payout. Weekly batch eligible cash per traveller; no open dispute release, verify actual payout status and available balance. No real-money implementation until approvals.
