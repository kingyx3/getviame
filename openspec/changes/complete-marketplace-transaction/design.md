# Design — Complete funded marketplace transaction (v0.12 draft)
Read docs/product/direct-charge-acceptance-v0.11.md, owner-answers-v0.11.md, wallet-policy.md, decisions.md and engineering/architecture.md. Single Stripe Connect direct charges, SGD PayNow-only, no FX. Prefer Stripe-managed pricing/risk, hosted onboarding, stable API and dedicated verified individual merchant accounts with exclusive platform payout control; precise settings and provider/legal permission blocked. No escrow, pooled platform customer funds or wallets.
State: selected/awaiting_funding -> funded (verified direct charge + eligible backed promotion) -> purchased -> actual delivery report -> confirmed -> dispute clearance -> settlement eligible -> payout reserved -> payout pending -> paid. Payment, refund, cancellation, evidence and payout states orthogonal; immutable original merchant, charge and order. Signed Connect webhook, authoritative retrieval, transactional guards, balanced journal, deduped outbox and provider idempotency keys, reconcile unknown outcomes before retry. Verify account/currency/amount/environment and control fingerprint before accepting; no duplicate funded assignment on checkout expiry/replay.
Receipt mandatory private with approved exception; uploader/staff access, price observations internal. Serialize receipt submission vs cancellation. 30-day acceptance-to-delivery max; report actual handover, immediate prompt/day3/day6 reminders, eligible silent auto-confirm day7, distinct confirmation +7 day dispute, early disputes allowed; no-show/contested evidence blocks, never backdate. AI advises; human approves exceptional finance.
Fee F=max(100, half-up(9% gross cents)); normal seller G-F. Application fee adjusted for actual processor costs (including approved tax treatment), separate fee refunds and platform reimbursements to preserve exact promised net. Before valid purchase receipt, full original-method refund and restore eligible promos; after valid receipt, penalty P=half-up(20% G), F gross fee, P-F seller, G-P refund allocated proportionately to original tenders. Apply stable largest-remainder cent allocation and approved grant restoration grace per wallet-policy.md; provider/legal verification pending; no promo cash conversion. Processor costs funded separately by platform. No automatic pro-rata application fee reversal unless matches policy. S$100 cash example 1.30 collection, app fee7.70, seller91; full refund returns app fee and adds1.30 of platform-owned funds (verified ordering/availability before refund); partial refund80 leaves11, retains7.70. Do not rely on future revenue or merchant funding; test cold start, insufficiency and after-payout recovery via approved fallback. Explicit lawful cancellation disclosures; no penalty on invalid/late receipt. No early normal settlement; cancellation compensation requires cleared approved cancellation/dispute handling.
Traveller cancellation: original-tender refund (pending until verified), reopen eligible request unfunded; replacement fresh agreement and independently confirmed direct charge, no reuse or reassignment. Refund permissions and account deauthorisation/offboarding fallback required. Merchant-initiated refund is reconciled and escalated without duplicate; no attempt to block lawful consumer rights. Late/failed refund approved verified-payer exception with maker/checker and no double refund; no automatic alternate payout.
Manual payout exclusively under platform control, no merchant schedule changes, manual/instant/API or alternate bypass. Check current config before new funding and payout; alerts/quarantine on drift, account status changes or unavailable verification. Batch only eligible amounts per connected account weekly, subtract ineligible obligations, pending refunds and approved reserves from reconciled available funds. Never treat total available balance as eligible. Serialize account/order reservations; reconcile external merchant/provider activity before payout; insufficient/unmatched balances block affected payout and escalate, never erase refund liabilities. Retention cutoff must not trigger automatic seller release of disputed funds; approved resolution path before deadline. Record actual bank payout status. No real-money implementation before approval; gates in PAY-001–013 and acceptance matrix are mandatory, provider-side behavior needs evidence beyond mocks.

Code/manual/validated evidence, no unsupported auto-confirm; dispute/appeal targets and rights, private transaction history. Separate authorised sandbox evaluation (R-006A) from production implementation and acceptance. No live operation without separate authority.
