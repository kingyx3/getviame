# Design — Complete funded marketplace transaction

Draft, v0.2; see domain-model.md, architecture.md, agent-operations.md, metrics.md and decision register.

SGD-only contracts, payment, transfer and payout; no Stripe FX. Platform SGD wallet desired; candidate licensed provider balance plus non-spendable obligations journal, no customer stored value without D-013 clearance; Stripe not escrow. PayNow Connect charge type, MoR, KYC, liability, refund horizon and payout timing pending. Fund after acceptance before purchase, signed provider events, immutable balanced journal, outbox/reconciliation, idempotency. Mandatory receipt unless documented exception; uploader and staff access; extracted prices internal.
Meetup: traveller reports actual delivery, evidence and server time; shopper notified/reminded, confirms or disputes. Proposed one concurrent 7-day clock from eligible actual report (not planned date), no early payout, no response at deadline auto-confirm absent dispute/evidence exception. Clock, evidence and cadence pending D-008/009. No-show not delivery. AI recommends, authorized human approves dispute finance.
Shopper free cancellation before purchase, after purchase mutual consent/dispute and possible disclosed penalty (schedule/trigger pending). Traveller cancel tracks rate and reopens eligible request; proposed original refund then new offer/payment; never auto-assign/reuse funds without approval. Revised cost requires consent or cancel; traveller bears actual lawful costs. Immutable snapshots/history.
