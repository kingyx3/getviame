## ADDED Requirements


### Requirement: SAFE-001 Protect participants
The system SHALL provide reporting, blocking, rate limits, moderation of abuse/counterfeit/IP/unsafe meetup, progressive restrictions for repeated unjustified cancellations by either role without new monetary penalties; preserve obligations.

#### Scenario: Report
- **WHEN** harassment or counterfeit reported
- **THEN** review queue and protected evidence

#### Scenario: Block
- **WHEN** blocked user messages
- **THEN** deny except approved obligation resolution

#### Scenario: Repeat
- **WHEN** review confirms repeated unjustified cancellations
- **THEN** apply approved restrictions with audit and review
