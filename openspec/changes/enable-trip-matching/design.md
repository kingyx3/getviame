# Design — Enable traveller discovery and offers

Draft, v0.2; see domain-model.md, architecture.md, agent-operations.md, metrics.md and decision register.

Singapore round-trip focus, generic origin/destination legs and dates, optional city/airport; no luggage capacity. Deterministic explainable matching and privacy; group similar opportunities while separate orders. SGD quote snapshot, inclusive 8% fee, net receipt, optional indicative home-currency display (source/time), public MSRP/cost/tax assumptions optional. Never override chosen price, never invoke Stripe FX. Traveller bears lawful expenses incl GST/duty, keeps savings, acknowledges T&C before accepted commitment. Revised price requires shopper consent/new funding handling or cancellation. Advance requests allowed; delivery deadline at most 30 days from acceptance and subject to stricter provider limit. Atomic acceptance one active full-quantity order, competing offers and counteroffers; no self dealing.
