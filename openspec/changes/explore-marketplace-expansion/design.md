# Design — Explore gated marketplace expansion

Draft, v0.2; see domain-model.md, architecture.md, agent-operations.md, metrics.md and decision register.

Customer/earnings wallets removed from MVP, no future wallet commitment. Any new stored value, top-ups, card authorisation or new corridors require separate founder decision and provider/counsel clearance. Future card direction is authorise at selection and capture at valid purchase receipt; expiry and failed-capture liability unresolved. No real money before approval. Future corridors and currencies explicitly gated, no launch Stripe FX. Partner opt-in permitted itinerary imports both ways; food/commercial imports, courier proof/cost/liability gated. Separate bookmarks and collective purchase proposals; shared allocation/refunds require explicit policy.
